The Story Behind
MA Labs

Hi, I'm Catherine. Before founding MA Labs, I spent [Number] years working in [Your Industry/Background, e.g., robotics and mechanical engineering], building tools designed to make human life a little bit easier.

But the real inspiration for MA Labs didn't come from a lab. It came from my Abuela. Growing up, I watched her stubbornly insist on doing her own grocery shopping at the local market. She loved the independence, the fresh air, and chatting with the vendors. But as she got older, carrying the heavy bags back home became a struggle that slowly started to rob her of that daily joy.

I realized that true mobility isn't just about wheelchairs or vehicles—it's about removing the literal burdens that stop people from living their lives actively and vibrantly. I built the first prototype of our cart in my garage so my Abuela could walk freely, unburdened, with her "helpful neighbor" carrying the load right beside her.

Today, MA Labs is dedicated to that exact mission: keeping goods—and people—moving with dignity, joy, and practical design.
